ISO/IEC 42001:2023 · AI management system

ISO 42001 Product Map

How the standard's clauses and Annex A controls map to features and technical components of a product that governs and secures AI applications for private organisations. Each row gives the requirement, the product feature that serves it, how to build it, and the evidence an auditor will ask to see.

The idea

Three layers to build

ISO 42001 asks an organisation to run a management system: policies, risk and impact assessments, controls, monitoring, and continual improvement. A product helps by making each of those a workflow, enforcing the technical controls at runtime, and collecting the evidence automatically.

Governance layer

AI inventory, policies, roles, risk and impact assessments, approvals, Statement of Applicability. Mostly workflow software.

Enforcement layer

A gateway or SDK in front of models and agents that applies policy: data filtering, prompt-injection defence, tool permissions, rate and cost limits.

Evidence layer

Tamper-evident logs, monitoring metrics, incident records and audit exports that prove the controls ran.

Map the same controls to NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage), the OWASP LLM and Agentic Top 10, and the EU AI Act in one internal control library. Customers can then reuse one piece of evidence across several frameworks, which is what buyers of these products value most.

Build order

Suggested phases

  1. Inventory and gateway. AI discovery, AI-BOM, and a gateway with logging, PII redaction and prompt-injection detection. This gives immediate security value and the raw evidence everything else needs.
  2. Governance workflows. Policy library, risk register, impact assessments, SoA generator and owner assignment. This covers most of clauses 5, 6 and A.2 to A.5.
  3. Lifecycle gates. CI/CD integration, eval pipeline, release approvals, model cards. This covers clause 8 and A.6.
  4. Assurance. KPI dashboards, internal audit module, CAPA, management review packs and audit export. This is what lets a customer pass certification.
  5. Cross-framework mapping. NIST AI RMF, OWASP and EU AI Act views over the same controls and evidence.

Annex A control groups and clause summaries here are paraphrased. Buy the standard from ISO or your national body and build your control library from its exact wording, since auditors check against that text.
